## Summary

Google launched Gemini 3.5 Live Translate on June 9, 2026 — a real-time speech-to-speech translation model that supports over 70 languages and 2,000+ language combinations. Unlike prior systems that required English as an intermediary and supported only 5 languages, Live Translate processes streaming audio continuously, staying just a few seconds behind the speaker while preserving intonation, pacing, and pitch. All generated audio is watermarked with SynthID for AI detection.

## Key Highlights

- Supports 70+ languages and 2,000+ language pairs in a single meeting session
- Streaming architecture: translates continuously as speech arrives, not after pauses
- Preserves speaker-specific vocal characteristics: intonation, pacing, pitch
- Automatic language detection — no manual configuration required
- Robust to noisy environments
- Available in Gemini Live API (public preview), Google Meet (private preview), Google Translate on Android/iOS

## Why It Matters

Live Translate's jump from 5 languages to 70+ with sub-second latency and vocal preservation marks a qualitative shift in real-time multilingual communication, with strong enterprise and consumer product implications.
