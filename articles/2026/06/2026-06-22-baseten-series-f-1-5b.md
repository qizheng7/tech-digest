## Summary

Baseten announced a $1.5B Series F at a $13B valuation, co-led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction Partners, and Spark Capital. The round — their fourth fundraise in 18 months — comes amid 20x revenue growth and 40x inference volume increase over the past year. Baseten is betting that enterprises want to own their intelligence layer by running open or specialized models with custom post-training rather than outsourcing to closed API providers.

## Key Highlights

- $1.5B raised at $13B valuation; co-led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction Partners, Spark Capital; additional investors include Greylock, IVP, Blackbird, D.E. Shaw Ventures, and others
- Revenue grew 20x and inference volume grew 40x over the past year
- Customer base includes Cursor, Notion, Lovable, Harvey, HubSpot, OpenEvidence, Abridge, and Decagon
- Thesis: enterprises increasingly want to own their intelligence layer — run open/specialized models, post-train on proprietary data, and retain control over continual learning
- Fourth fundraise in 18 months, reflecting sustained hypergrowth in the inference infrastructure market

## Why It Matters

Baseten's Series F signals that the inference infrastructure layer is consolidating into a standalone high-value category, with enterprises willing to invest in owned model serving rather than relying solely on closed API providers.
