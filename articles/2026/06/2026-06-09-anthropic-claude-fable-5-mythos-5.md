## Summary

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on June 9, 2026 — two Mythos-class models sharing the same underlying weights but differing in safety safeguards. Fable 5 is publicly available with classifiers that intercept cybersecurity, CBRN, and distillation-related requests, falling back to Opus 4.8 for less than 5% of sessions. Mythos 5 removes these safeguards and is restricted to Project Glasswing partners and authorized biomedical researchers. Both are priced at $10/M input and $50/M output — less than half the cost of Claude Mythos Preview.

## Key Highlights

- Benchmark-leading performance on Cognition's FrontierCode and Hebbia's Finance Benchmark
- 3× better than Opus 4.8 on memory-intensive long-context tasks across millions of tokens
- Stripe demo: completed a 50-million-line Ruby migration in one day vs. two months manually
- State-of-the-art vision: reconstructs web apps from screenshots, extracts data from scientific figures
- Mythos 5 accelerated protein design 10× and generated molecular biology hypotheses preferred 80% of the time by scientists
- 30-day data retention policy on all Mythos-class traffic for safety monitoring

## Why It Matters

Fable 5/Mythos 5 represents Anthropic's most direct attempt to balance frontier capability distribution with risk management through a tiered access model — a template likely to define how the industry handles dual-use AI going forward.
