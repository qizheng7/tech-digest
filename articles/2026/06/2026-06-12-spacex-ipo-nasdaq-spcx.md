## Summary

SpaceX listed on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX on June 12, 2026, raising $75 billion at a $1.75 trillion valuation — the largest IPO in stock market history. Priced at $135 per share (556.6 million shares offered), SPCX closed its first day at $161, a 19% gain. The IPO had been anticipated throughout 2026 following months of secondary market activity and came ahead of similar pending listings from OpenAI and Anthropic.

## Key Highlights

- Raised $75 billion at a **$1.75 trillion valuation** — the largest IPO in history
- Priced at $135/share; closed first day at **$161 (+19%)**
- Listed on Nasdaq under ticker **SPCX** on June 12, 2026
- OpenAI and Anthropic both filed confidential S-1s around the same time, signaling a wave of AI/tech mega-IPOs
- SpaceX executives rang the Nasdaq closing bell on debut day

## Why It Matters

SpaceX's successful $75B debut validates the appetite for multi-trillion-dollar tech listings and clears the path for OpenAI and Anthropic's anticipated IPOs — a potential reordering of the public market landscape around AI companies.
