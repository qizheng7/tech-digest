## Summary

Anthropic launched Claude Corps on June 11, 2026 — a $150 million national fellowship program that trains early-career workers (18+ with under two years of full-time experience) to use Claude effectively, then places them with nonprofits for a full year at $85,000 salary and benefits. The program aims to simultaneously equip the nonprofit sector with AI capabilities and build AI workforce skills in people who might otherwise be left behind by automation. CodePath manages fellows as employer; Social Finance handles measurement and scaling infrastructure.

## Key Highlights

- $150 million initial investment from Anthropic
- 1,000 fellows in the first cohort; pays $85,000 + benefits for one-year nonprofit placements
- Open to anyone 18+ with under two years of full-time work experience, no educational requirements
- Dual mission: AI-enable nonprofits + build AI workforce skills for at-risk early-career workers
- CodePath (employer of record + mentorship), Social Finance (measurement + future scaling)
- Framed explicitly as a response to AI-driven economic disruption

## Why It Matters

Claude Corps represents a significant bet that AI labs have a direct social responsibility to fund workforce transition programs — and positions Anthropic as an active participant in the labor displacement debate, not just a bystander.
