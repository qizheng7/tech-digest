## Summary

Fireworks made Qwen 3.7 Plus available on June 12, 2026 — Alibaba's latest multimodal agent model offering ~3.55× faster throughput than its predecessor Qwen 3.6 Plus. The model supports a 262K token context window, processes text and images natively, and offers switchable thinking/non-thinking modes. With reasoning enabled, it matches the flagship Qwen 3.7 Max on math benchmarks while running 3× faster. Fireworks pricing comes in ~50% cheaper than its predecessor on the platform.

## Key Highlights

- 3.55× faster throughput than Qwen 3.6 Plus
- 262K token context window; native image understanding alongside text
- Switchable reasoning: thinking mode matches Qwen Max on math; non-thinking mode for faster responses
- Fireworks pricing: $0.50/M input, $3.00/M output, $0.10/M cached, 50% batch discount
- ~50% cheaper than predecessor on Fireworks; designed for long-horizon agent workflows with screenshots and tool calls

## Why It Matters

Qwen 3.7 Plus shows that the frontier multimodal agent tier is becoming commodity-priced — strong reasoning at 3× speed for $0.50/M input sets a new cost-performance bar for open-weight alternatives.
