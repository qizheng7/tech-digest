## Summary

Google introduced Gemini 3.5 Flash at Google I/O 2026, the first model in the Gemini 3.5 family and a new flagship for agentic AI tasks. The model achieves frontier-level benchmarks at 4x the output token speed of comparable models, and a Gemini 3.5 Pro is expected next month. It is available via the Gemini app, Google Search, the Antigravity development platform, the Gemini API, and enterprise platforms. The model can generate interactive web interfaces, deploy parallel subagents, and handle complex multi-step coding and financial workflows.

## Key Highlights

- **Terminal-Bench 2.1**: 76.2% — outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding and agentic benchmarks
- **MCP Atlas**: 83.6%; **CharXiv Reasoning**: 84.2%
- **4x faster** output tokens per second than other frontier models
- Supports parallel subagent deployment for large-scale problem-solving
- Less than half the cost of competing models per comparable task
- Available today; Gemini 3.5 Pro coming next month

## Why It Matters

Gemini 3.5 Flash directly challenges the assumption that speed and reasoning are tradeoffs — delivering frontier benchmark results at inference speeds that make real-time agentic workflows economically viable at enterprise scale.
