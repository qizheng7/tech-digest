## Summary

OpenAI launched a self-serve Ads Manager (beta) that allows advertisers to create, manage, and optimize campaigns inside ChatGPT directly — without going through an agency partner. The platform supports both cost-per-click (CPC) and cost-per-impression (CPM) buying models and integrates with major agency holding companies and ad-tech partners including Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis, WPP, Adobe, Criteo, and StackAdapt. OpenAI stated that ads will not influence ChatGPT's organic outputs and introduced new privacy and measurement controls for advertisers.

## Key Highlights

- **Self-serve Ads Manager (beta)** enables advertisers to create and manage ChatGPT campaigns without agency intermediaries.
- Supports both **CPC (cost-per-click)** and **CPM (cost-per-impression)** buying models.
- **Agency integrations:** Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis, WPP; **ad-tech integrations:** Adobe, Criteo, StackAdapt.
- OpenAI is reportedly targeting **$2.5B in advertising revenue in 2026** and $100B annually by 2030.
- Explicit commitment that **ads will not influence ChatGPT's organic outputs** — separation of paid and organic results.
- Includes new privacy and measurement tools for advertiser attribution.

## Why It Matters

OpenAI entering self-serve advertising transforms ChatGPT from a pure subscription product into a dual-revenue platform, and signals that conversational AI is becoming the next major advertising medium — with OpenAI's scale (hundreds of millions of users) making it a credible rival to search-based ad platforms.
