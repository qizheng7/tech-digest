## Summary

Google launched Gemini for Science at I/O 2026 — a collection of AI-powered tools targeting scientific research via Google Labs. Three experimental tools were announced: Co-Scientist (multi-agent hypothesis generation with verified citations), AlphaEvolve + ERA (computational discovery that tests thousands of code variations in parallel), and NotebookLM (literature structuring and presentation). A Science Skills Bundle integrates 30+ life science databases (UniProt, AlphaFold, AlphaGenome) on the Antigravity platform, and papers on ERA and Co-Scientist have been published in Nature.

## Key Highlights

- **Co-Scientist**: Multi-agent "idea tournaments" generate and evaluate hypotheses with verified citations for accelerated scientific discovery
- **AlphaEvolve + ERA**: Tests thousands of code/model variations in parallel for fields like solar forecasting and materials science
- **NotebookLM integration**: Structures scientific papers into searchable tables and auto-generates reports/presentations
- **Science Skills Bundle**: Integrates 30+ life science databases (UniProt, AlphaFold, AlphaGenome) for bioinformatics on Antigravity platform
- Enterprise adoption in preview: BASF, Klarna, Daiichi Sankyo, Bayer Crop Science
- Research published in *Nature*: ERA and Co-Scientist peer-reviewed papers

## Why It Matters

Google is positioning Gemini as the AI layer for all of science — combining literature understanding, hypothesis generation, and computational search under one agent-powered platform, directly competing with specialized AI-for-science tools.
