## Summary

OpenAI confidentially filed its S-1 IPO prospectus with the SEC on May 22, 2026, marking one of the most anticipated tech IPOs in history. The company is targeting a Q4 2026 public debut — possibly as early as September — at a valuation between $852 billion and $1 trillion. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are leading the deal. The filing arrives despite OpenAI losing $1.22 for every $1 of revenue in Q1 2026.

## Key Highlights

- Confidential S-1 filed May 22, 2026; public S-1 must be published at least 15 days before the road show begins
- Target valuation: $852B–$1T; lead underwriters are Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley
- Q1 2026 financials: negative 122% non-GAAP operating margin — ~$6.95B quarterly operating loss
- Full-year 2025: $13.1B in revenue, ~$22B in costs, net loss ~$9B
- IPO target window: Q4 2026, possibly September 2026
- The filing comes shortly after the Axios May 20 report that OpenAI was preparing to file "as soon as May 22"
- SpaceX also filed its own S-1 around the same time, targeting $75B raise at $1.5T+ valuation

## Why It Matters

OpenAI going public is a generational market event — the S-1 will force unprecedented transparency into frontier AI economics at a moment when the company is still burning >$1 for every $1 earned, making the IPO a high-stakes test of whether investors will fund the long arc toward AGI profitability.
