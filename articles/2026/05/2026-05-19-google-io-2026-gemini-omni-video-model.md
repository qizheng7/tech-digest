## Summary

Google unveiled Gemini Omni at Google I/O 2026, a new multimodal model family capable of generating video from any input combination — text, images, audio, or video — grounded in Gemini's real-world knowledge. Unlike text-to-video systems like Veo, Omni's design is built on world-model research, enabling more accurate physics simulation (gravity, fluid dynamics, kinetic energy) and voice-controlled conversational editing of scenes. The first model, Gemini Omni Flash, is rolling out today with plans to expand to "any output from any input" over time.

## Key Highlights

- **Gemini Omni Flash** available today in Gemini app, Google Flow, and YouTube Shorts — free for YouTube Shorts users; paid tiers for Gemini app and Google Flow
- Conversational editing: change characters, backgrounds, and scene elements with voice commands
- Physics accuracy: simulates gravity, fluid dynamics, and kinetic energy, not just visual plausibility
- Built on world-model research; ultimately designed to generate any modality from any input
- All generated videos carry a **SynthID watermark** viewable in the Gemini app
- Currently limited to self-avatar videos; cross-person audio/speech editing still in safety testing

## Why It Matters

Gemini Omni represents Google's bid for the video-AI platform layer — integrating world-model understanding into consumer generation tools, with YouTube Shorts distribution putting it in front of billions of users on day one.
