## Summary

At Google I/O 2026, Google launched Gemini 3.5 Flash — available today across the Gemini app, Google Search, and the Gemini API — positioning it as "four times faster than other frontier models" for output token throughput. The model excels at agentic tasks and long-context coding, powering Gemini Spark, a new 24/7 personal AI agent. Gemini 3.5 Pro is set to roll out next month, and the broader model family is described as combining "frontier intelligence with action."

## Key Highlights

- **Available now**: Gemini 3.5 Flash live via Gemini app, Google Search, Antigravity, Gemini API, and enterprise platforms
- **Gemini 3.5 Pro** rolling out next month
- **4× faster** output token throughput than other frontier models
- Benchmark scores: Terminal-Bench 2.1 — 76.2%; GDPval-AA — 1656 Elo; MCP Atlas — 83.6%; CharXiv Reasoning (multimodal) — 84.2%
- Powers **Gemini Spark**: personal AI agent running 24/7, integrating with Sheets, Gmail — rolling out to AI Ultra subscribers ($100/mo)
- AI Ultra pricing restructured: new tier at $100; existing $250 plan drops to $200

## Why It Matters

Google is positioning Gemini 3.5 as the speed-frontier model for production agentic workloads, directly competing with Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's GPT-5.5 on the emerging coding-agent and enterprise platforms market.
