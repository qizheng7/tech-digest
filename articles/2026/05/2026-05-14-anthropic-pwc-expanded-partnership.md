## Summary

Anthropic expanded its strategic alliance with PwC, described as "the deepest commitment within our Claude Partner Network." PwC will roll out Claude Code and Cowork starting with U.S. teams, scaling to hundreds of thousands of professionals globally. A joint Center of Excellence was established, and PwC is certifying 30,000 professionals on Claude. Existing deployments have delivered up to 70% improvements in delivery times.

## Key Highlights

- Deploying Claude Code + Cowork across PwC's hundreds of thousands of professionals globally
- Joint Center of Excellence + certification program for 30,000 PwC professionals
- Use cases: agentic technology development, AI-native deal execution (M&A diligence, value creation), and enterprise function reinvention (finance, supply chain, HR, engineering)
- New "Office of the CFO" business group focused on transforming client finance organizations
- Real results: insurance underwriting 10 weeks → 10 days; cybersecurity incident response hours → minutes
- Advocate Health deploying toward all 167,000 staff

## Why It Matters

PwC's at-scale deployment of Claude Code into professional services workflows is one of the largest real-world enterprise rollouts of an AI coding agent, validating the agentic development model beyond software-native companies.
