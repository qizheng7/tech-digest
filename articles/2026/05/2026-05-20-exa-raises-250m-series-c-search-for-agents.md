## Summary

Exa, the AI-native search startup that organizes web data for AI agents and LLM applications, raised $250 million in Series C funding at a $2.2 billion valuation, led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z). The round follows an $85M Series B less than a year prior and reflects surging demand from developers and enterprises building agentic systems that need reliable, structured web retrieval. Exa currently serves over 5,000 companies and 400,000 developers, with customers including HubSpot, Cursor, Cognition, OpenRouter, and Monday.com.

## Key Highlights

- **$250M Series C at $2.2B valuation** — led by Andreessen Horowitz, with prior investors including NVIDIA and Y Combinator.
- Exa provides **semantic search APIs optimized for AI agents** — retrieving structured, citation-ready web content rather than ranked blue links.
- Serves **5,000+ companies and 400,000+ developers**, with notable customers: HubSpot, Cursor, Cognition, OpenRouter, Monday.com.
- Plans to use funds to **train next-generation search models** and scale infrastructure to handle hundreds of thousands of searches per second.
- Exa explicitly frames itself as "a search lab organizing the web's data for agents" — distinct from consumer search engines.
- Raised $85M Series B less than 12 months prior, suggesting extreme revenue or usage growth.

## Why It Matters

As agentic AI moves from demos to production, reliable web retrieval becomes a critical infrastructure layer — Exa's rapid funding trajectory signals that the market is converging on purpose-built agent search as a distinct category from both consumer search and traditional RAG pipelines.
