## Summary

Hark, the AI hardware and foundation model startup founded by serial entrepreneur Brett Adcock (previously Figure.AI and Archer Aviation), raised $700 million in Series A funding at a $6 billion post-money valuation. The round was led by Parkway Venture Capital with participation from NVIDIA, AMD Ventures, Intel Capital, Qualcomm Ventures, Salesforce Ventures, ARK Invest, Brookfield, and others. Founded in late 2025 with $100M of Adcock's personal capital, Hark is building multimodal AI foundation models and custom hardware devices targeting a "universal interface" between humans and machines. The company plans to release its first AI models in summer 2026, followed by custom hardware.

## Key Highlights

- **$700M Series A at $6B post-money valuation** — one of the largest Series A rounds in AI hardware history.
- Led by **Parkway Venture Capital**; strategic investors include NVIDIA, AMD Ventures, Intel Capital, Qualcomm Ventures, and Salesforce Ventures — signaling hardware supply chain alignment.
- Brett Adcock previously founded **Figure.AI** (humanoid robotics) and **Archer Aviation** (electric aircraft) and seeded Hark with $100M of personal capital in late 2025.
- **~70 employees** and an operational NVIDIA B200 data center for model training.
- Strategy: build foundation models, software systems, native hardware, and new interfaces together from the start — rather than retrofitting models onto commodity hardware.
- **First models planned for summer 2026**, followed by consumer and enterprise hardware devices.

## Why It Matters

Hark's raise signals that the market believes a "full-stack" AI company — one controlling its own models, software, and hardware — is viable at the frontier, with strategic chip investors betting that purpose-built hardware will differentiate the next generation of personal AI.
