## Summary

Anthropic announced a $200 million, four-year partnership with the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation spanning global health, life sciences, education, and economic mobility. The commitment includes grant funding, Claude usage credits, and technical support for programs in the US and globally. This is part of Anthropic's beneficial deployments initiative — extending AI "in areas where markets alone will not."

## Key Highlights

- $200M total: grant funding + Claude usage credits + technical support over 4 years
- Global health: accelerate vaccine/therapy development; improve health data access for ~4.6B people lacking essential health services
- Life sciences: screen drug/vaccine candidates for neglected diseases (polio, HPV, preeclampsia)
- Education: AI tutoring, career guidance, and literacy tools for K-12 in US, sub-Saharan Africa, and India
- Economic mobility: agricultural productivity tools for smallholder farmers; job-skills records/career guidance in the US
- Will create public goods including datasets and evaluation benchmarks

## Why It Matters

The Gates Foundation partnership signals that frontier AI labs are committing meaningful resources to global development use cases, and that Claude's capabilities are being tested on some of the hardest humanitarian problems.
