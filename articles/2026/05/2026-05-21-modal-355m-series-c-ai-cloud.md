## Summary

Modal raised $355M in Series C funding at a $4.65B post-money valuation, led by General Catalyst and Redpoint, with Menlo, Bain Capital Ventures, and Accel joining as new investors. The company has grown 5x since its September 2024 raise and now surpasses $300M in annualized revenue. Modal is rebuilding the cloud stack specifically for AI workloads — serverless containers, low-latency inference, RL training loops, and parallel sandbox infrastructure.

## Key Highlights

- $355M Series C at $4.65B valuation; 5x growth since September 2024, $300M+ ARR
- Led by General Catalyst and Redpoint; Menlo, Bain Capital Ventures, Accel are new investors; all major existing investors doubled down
- Core focus: low-latency inference primitives, RL training loops, parallel sandbox infrastructure, developer experience
- Founded by Erik Bernhardsson and Akshat Bubna; HQ in New York with offices in SF and Stockholm
- Positioned as "AI-native cloud" — rebuilding compute layer from scratch for modern AI workloads vs. retrofitting traditional cloud
- AINews community noted Modal alongside TurboPuffer and Exa as "New AI Infra Unicorns" after all three hit major milestones the same week

## Why It Matters

Modal's rapid ascent to $300M+ ARR demonstrates that AI-native cloud infrastructure is emerging as its own high-value category — companies are willing to pay a premium for compute platforms built ground-up for GPU workloads, inference, and agent execution rather than adapted from general-purpose cloud.
