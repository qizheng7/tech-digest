## Summary

DeepSeek announced that the 75% promotional discount on its flagship V4-Pro API is now permanent. What was set to expire May 31, 2026 will instead remain the official pricing indefinitely. The move places DeepSeek V4-Pro at $0.435/M input and $0.87/M output — approximately 12x cheaper than GPT-5.5 and 19x cheaper than Claude Opus 4.7 per Artificial Analysis estimates — while also permanently cutting input cache hit pricing by 90% across the entire API.

## Key Highlights

- New permanent pricing: $0.435/M input, $0.87/M output, $0.0036/M cached input (blended ~$0.18/M tokens)
- Output tokens priced "34x below GPT-5.5" according to third-party analysis
- ~12x cheaper than GPT-5.5 and ~19x cheaper than Claude Opus 4.7 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index benchmarks
- Input cache hit pricing cut 90% across entire DeepSeek API — makes repetitive prompts and system instructions nearly free
- V4-Pro sits on the Pareto frontier for intelligence vs. run cost per Artificial Analysis; the community reaction framed it as a push toward "intelligence too cheap to meter"
- The decision follows user backlash against more restrictive or expensive Western AI APIs; paired with legacy API deprecations (deepseek-chat, deepseek-reasoner) scheduled for July 24, 2026

## Why It Matters

DeepSeek's permanent price cut materially redraws the cost/performance frontier for LLM APIs — forcing Western labs competing on price to respond, and accelerating the industry's shift from model-quality competition to agent-experience and infrastructure differentiation.
