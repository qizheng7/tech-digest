## Summary

Anthropic and KPMG formed a global strategic alliance embedding Claude across KPMG's entire business and client services, reaching over 276,000 employees in 138 countries. Claude is integrated into KPMG's Digital Gateway platform (its core client work software), and Anthropic designates KPMG as preferred partner for private equity deployments. The deal comes after two years of KPMG US adoption and builds on the PwC partnership announced in May 2026, establishing a clear pattern of the Big Four professional services firms going all-in on Claude.

## Key Highlights

- 276,000+ employees in 138 countries gain Claude access through Digital Gateway
- Use cases: tax/legal services, cybersecurity remediation, code modernization via KPMG Blaze (Claude Code), AI agent creation for regulatory change
- Tax regulation AI agent development time dropped from weeks to minutes using Cowork + Managed Agents
- Anthropic designates KPMG as preferred partner for PE portfolio company deployments
- Joint product development planned for PE-focused AI workflows
- Two years of prior adoption in KPMG US before global rollout

## Why It Matters

The Big Four are now collectively standardizing on Claude as their AI backbone, giving Anthropic a dominant enterprise services channel that reaches the majority of Fortune 500 clients.
