## Summary

Cursor released Composer 2.5, their in-house coding agent model built on Kimi K2.5 (Moonshot AI's open-source checkpoint) and trained with 25× more synthetic RL tasks than Composer 2, achieving performance matching Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 at roughly 1/10th the cost. Simultaneously, Cursor disclosed a much larger strategic move: a partnership with SpaceXAI to train a significantly larger model from scratch using Colossus 2 (one million H100-equivalents) and 10× more total compute, using Muon optimizer and continued pretraining techniques.

## Key Highlights

- Base checkpoint: Moonshot AI's open-source Kimi K2.5; trained with 25× more synthetic coding tasks than Composer 2
- Matches Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 on coding benchmarks at a fraction of the price
- Pricing: $0.50/$2.50 per M tokens (standard); $3/$15 (fast interactive tier)
- Doubled usage limits for all users for the first week post-launch (~through May 25)
- SpaceXAI partnership: training a larger model from scratch on Colossus 2 (1M H100-equiv), 10× compute — no release date yet
- Training uses Muon optimizer and reward-hacking-resistant synthetic data pipelines

## Why It Matters

Cursor is the first AI coding IDE to train its own frontier-scale model, signaling a vertical integration trend where application layer companies own the model stack rather than just orchestrating third-party APIs.
