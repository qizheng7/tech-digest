## Summary

Anthropic launched Claude for Small Business, a package integrating Claude into tools small businesses already use — including QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365. The offering includes 15 ready-to-run agentic workflows covering finance, operations, sales, and customer service, all requiring user approval before execution. The initiative targets a gap: small businesses represent 44% of U.S. GDP but have lagged behind enterprises in AI adoption.

## Key Highlights

- 15 pre-built agentic workflows: payroll planning, invoice management, lead triage, campaign analysis, contract review, cash-flow forecasting, and more
- Integrates via toggle-install into 7+ major SMB platforms (QuickBooks, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, PayPal)
- All tasks require human approval before execution — trust-first design
- Paired with "AI Fluency for Small Business" course and nationwide SMB Tour
- Partnerships with nonprofits serving underrepresented entrepreneurs
- Part of a broader push via Anthropic's beneficial deployments initiative

## Why It Matters

Anthropic is expanding Claude's addressable market from enterprises and developers down to the long tail of SMBs, betting that workflow automation with guardrails is the right entry point for non-technical business owners.
