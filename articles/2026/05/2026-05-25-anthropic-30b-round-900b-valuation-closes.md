## Summary

Anthropic is closing a $30 billion funding round at a $900 billion pre-money valuation — its second $30B raise in 14 weeks, after a February 2026 Series G at a $380B valuation. Sequoia Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Altimeter Capital are co-leading the round, each contributing at least $2 billion. The fundraise came together in roughly two weeks after investor outreach began, an unusually compressed timeline that reflects Anthropic's explosive revenue trajectory: annualized revenue is now projected to exceed $45 billion, up from a $9B pace at year-end 2025, driven primarily by Claude Code's adoption.

## Key Highlights

- $30B round closing end of May 2026 at $900B+ pre-money valuation, surpassing OpenAI's ~$852B valuation
- Four co-leads (Sequoia, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Altimeter) each investing $2B+; existing investors including Founders Fund and General Catalyst also participating
- Raises Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate to $45B+, with Claude Code as the primary growth driver
- Entire fundraise negotiated in ~2 weeks — extraordinarily fast for a round this size
- Second $30B raise in 14 weeks (previous: February 2026 at $380B post-money valuation)
- Anthropic revenues now appear to surpass OpenAI's, though the two companies use different accounting methods

## Why It Matters

Anthropic's valuation doubling in 14 weeks signals that Claude Code's enterprise penetration has converted AI hype into sustained, measurable revenue — making Anthropic the most valuable private AI company in the world heading into IPO season.
