## Summary

OpenAI is preparing for a public offering targeting September 2026, working with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as lead banking advisors. CEO Sam Altman may file IPO paperwork confidentially with regulators within days or weeks, with the company expected to be valued at up to $1 trillion and targeting a raise of at least $60 billion. The announcement came one day after Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI was dismissed, and coincides with SpaceX also pursuing a simultaneous IPO filing.

## Key Highlights

- Confidential IPO filing with regulators expected "within days or weeks"; public debut potentially as early as September 2026
- Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley serving as lead banking advisors
- Valuation target: up to $1 trillion; minimum raise target: ~$60 billion
- Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI was dismissed on May 18, 2026 — one day before the announcement
- SpaceX (now a major AI competitor after xAI acquisition) pursuing a parallel IPO, framing OpenAI vs. SpaceX as the next Musk-Altman battleground
- OpenAI declined to comment on IPO plans

## Why It Matters

A $1 trillion OpenAI IPO would be one of the largest in history, potentially setting the valuation floor for the entire frontier AI sector and determining how public markets price AI capabilities companies.
