## Summary

Anthropic closed a $65 billion Series H round, reaching a $965 billion post-money valuation — up from $900 billion just three weeks prior. The round was co-led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia, with participation from Capital Group, Coatue, D1 Capital Partners, GIC, ICONIQ, Fidelity, BlackRock, General Catalyst, Lightspeed, Amazon ($5B), Micron, Samsung, and SK hynix. Monthly run-rate revenue recently crossed $47 billion. The capital will fund safety research, compute infrastructure expansion, and scaling of products including Claude Code and Claude Cowork, with major compute agreements secured: Amazon (5 GW), Google/Broadcom (5 GW TPU capacity), and SpaceX.

## Key Highlights

- **$65B round** at **$965B post-money valuation**, from $900B three weeks earlier
- **Monthly run-rate revenue** crossed $47 billion
- **Compute agreements**: 5 GW from Amazon, 5 GW TPU capacity from Google/Broadcom, plus SpaceX
- **Hardware investors**: Micron, Samsung, SK hynix — unusual supply-chain participation
- **Claude available** across all three major cloud platforms (AWS, Google, Azure)
- Capital earmarked for safety research, compute infra, and scaling Claude Code / Cowork products

## Why It Matters

At $965 billion valuation, Anthropic is within striking distance of a $1T valuation ahead of a potential IPO, and the breadth of strategic investors (cloud hyperscalers, memory chipmakers, institutional funds) signals that the entire AI supply chain is locking in alignment with Claude's trajectory.
