## Summary

Google announced Gemini Omni Flash at Google I/O 2026, the first model in a new Omni family designed for multimodal creation starting with video. Users can create and edit videos through natural language prompts that build sequentially while maintaining character consistency and physics-grounded rendering (gravity, kinetic energy, fluid dynamics). The model accepts images, audio, video, and text as combined reference inputs. It is available to Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers via the Gemini app and Google Flow, with free access on YouTube Shorts. All outputs include SynthID watermarking.

## Key Highlights

- **Conversational video editing**: natural language prompts edit sequentially while maintaining consistency
- **Physics-grounded generation**: improved understanding of gravity, kinetic energy, and fluid dynamics
- **Multi-input support**: combine image + audio + video + text as references for cohesive outputs
- Available on Gemini app, Google Flow, and YouTube Shorts (free tier)
- **SynthID watermarking** on all outputs for content verification
- Pairs with Gemini's real-world knowledge for storytelling and educational use

## Why It Matters

Omni Flash marks Google's expansion from image generation into video creation with physics awareness, representing a step toward AI that understands and generates the physical world rather than just static content.
