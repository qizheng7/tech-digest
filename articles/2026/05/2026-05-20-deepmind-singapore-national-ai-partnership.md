## Summary

Google DeepMind announced a broad national AI partnership with Singapore at Google I/O 2026, spanning healthcare, education, sustainability, and responsible AI. Key commitments include an AI Co-Clinician initiative with public health clusters, Gemini for Education rollout to all primary-through-JC educators, a $7M Google.org grant for pandemic preparedness using AlphaFold and Google Earth, and the launch of a "Google DeepMind Accelerator: AI for the Planet" across Asia-Pacific. The partnership is projected to generate S$3.3 billion (US$2.5B) in additional economic value by 2040.

## Key Highlights

- **Healthcare**: AI Co-Clinician initiative exploring "triadic care" (AI + patient + doctor); $7M Google.org grant for infectious disease research with AlphaFold
- **Education**: Gemini for Education deployed to all Singapore educators from primary schools to junior colleges; teacher AI upskilling with the Ministry of Education
- **Accessibility**: Gemma-powered running assistant for blind and low-vision athletes, tested with SG Enable
- **Sustainability**: "Google DeepMind Accelerator: AI for the Planet" launching across Asia-Pacific for startups/nonprofits tackling climate challenges
- **Responsible AI**: Partnership with IMDA and MLCommons on multilingual and multimodal safety benchmarks
- Projected economic impact: S$3.3 billion by 2040

## Why It Matters

This partnership is a template for Google DeepMind's strategy of converting frontier AI research into national-scale deployments in key Asia-Pacific markets, while building regulatory goodwill through responsible AI benchmarking collaborations.
